  queued behind a `claimed` head whose own open PR is red. Comment-only, on
  the head alone, naming the remedy — service the red, or re-order the chain
  (#440, #426).

  tripwire grades a head with the same instrument that decides
  `blocker:ci-red` and the two can never disagree (#440, #136, #139, #208).
